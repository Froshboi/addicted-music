About Addicted Music

Founded in 2025 out of Port Harcourt, Addicted Music is a trap label and artist management outfit built around one belief: the room comes before the release.

We handle the business side so the artists we manage — starting with Mosidon — can stay focused on the studio. No shortcuts, no ghostwritten trends, just PHC sound made properly and put out on our own terms.

What you hear on Audiomack today is the first chapter. There's more coming out of the console.
